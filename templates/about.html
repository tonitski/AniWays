OUR STORY

What's AniWays?

A convenient way to connect agricultural products, people, and possibilities.

What's AniWays?

The name AniWays combines the Filipino word “Ani,” which means harvest, and “Ways,” which means convenient ways to access agricultural products. The name AniWays represents our online agribusiness marketplace, where people can buy and sell different agricultural products in one place. Instead of focusing on only one product, AniWays provides opportunities for farmers, sellers, and consumers to connect through a variety of agricultural goods. Our concept is simple: whatever agricultural products you want to sell or buy, there are many ways to make it happen with AniWays.

Our Mission

Make agricultural products easier to discover by connecting farmers, sellers, and consumers through an accessible online marketplace.

AniVision

Grow into a trusted digital community where Filipino agriculture, local enterprise, and everyday shoppers thrive together.

AniGoals

Offer variety, support local sellers, improve convenience, and make farm essentials easier to find.
